Digital Identity

voltage

This is a digital drawing, I decide to make a semi-self portrait while exaggerating and altering my appearance to fit my art style. I am a huge fan of Frankenstein the book and the story, it has been retold so many different ways. I used Adobe illustrator to create this piece, it made me simplify my work in a way while also refining it. I think with this form of a self portrait you are able to see more than what I look like.
